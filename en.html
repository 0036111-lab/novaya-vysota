Vera Navalikhina is a park skateboarder from Moscow, a Russian Master of Sport and three-time national champion.

At nine, she placed second in the Russian U16 Championship and third in the senior national championship. Her titles in 2023, 2024 and 2025 continued several years of consistent progress.

Why Vera

Vera has a strong starting position for LA 2028.

At 16, she has already progressed from her first medals to three national titles and international World Skate events. The next step is to strengthen technique, ranking and top-level competition experience.

Her journey is bigger than one result: it can introduce a new generation of Russian action-sports athletes to the Olympic stage.

About New Heights

From underground culture
to Olympic sport.
From riders to champions.

Russia’s board-sports culture grew in the 1990s through independent scenes, pioneering riders, DIY spots and communities. Today skateboarding is Olympic, and the first generation is passing its experience forward.

Vera’s father, Mikhail “Smol” Navalikhine, was part of that first wave and, according to Sovetsky Sport, Russia’s first sponsored skateboarder. He later became a coach and co-created family action-sports camps.

Vera’s mother, Elena, founded the Kidsproriders school and manages her daughter’s athletic pathway. Vera grew up inside this culture and now carries the family story into national championships and international events.

New Heights helps action-sports culture become a system by connecting athletes and communities with business and public institutions.
