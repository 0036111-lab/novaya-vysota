Vera Navalikhina
× New Heights

A development programme supporting an emerging Russian park skateboarder through the international LA 2028 Olympic cycle.

Vera in one paragraph

Sixteen years old, Moscow, women’s park. Russian Master of Sport, national champion in 2023, 2024 and 2025, and a World Skate Tour and World Skate Games competitor.

First stage

A three-week high-performance programme at LVLUP Academy in California: technique, bowl lines, new tricks, run consistency and international competition preparation.

What a partner receives

A structured Olympic pathway

Objectives, stages, team and preparation plan.

An authentic story

Photography, video and content from the athlete’s journey.

Transparent results

Programme and financial reporting in an agreed format.

Ways to participate

  • Fund a preparation stage
  • Flights and logistics
  • Equipment and sports services
  • Medical and recovery support
  • Media and communications partnership
  • Expert support for the programme

About New Heights

New Heights helps action-sports culture become a system by connecting athletes and communities with business and public institutions — from the independent riders of the 1990s to a new generation of champions.
